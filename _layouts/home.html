About

About

I am a Knowledge Engineer and Neurosymbolic AI researcher with a PhD in Computer Science. My work focuses on designing knowledge representations and AI systems that connect heterogeneous data, structured semantics and neural models.

I have worked across multiple domains, including clinical operations, personal service robotics, restricted-access research data and metadata-driven data integration. Across these settings, the common challenge has been the same: how to represent complex knowledge in a way that supports interoperability, reasoning and reuse.

I currently work at UMC Utrecht, where I design knowledge architectures and semantic models for clinical operations. Previously, at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, I developed neurosymbolic AI approaches combining knowledge graphs, ontologies, multimodal models and robotic perception. My PhD research focused on semantic approaches for making restricted-access data more FAIR, discoverable and reusable.
