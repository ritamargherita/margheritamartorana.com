Selected Projects

Selected work spanning neurosymbolic robotics, semantic modeling and AI-assisted data integration.

Co-author · Neurosymbolic robotics

Bridging Bots

Combined multimodal language models with ontologies and knowledge graphs to turn robot perception into interoperable, ontology-compliant representations. Evaluation across five models showed that the integration strategy matters: newer models alone do not guarantee better results.

Multimodal AI Ontologies Robotics

Co-author · Ontology engineering

OntoBOT

Unified tasks, actions, environments and robot capabilities in an ontology for service robotics. Competency questions across four embodied agents demonstrated context-aware reasoning, task execution and knowledge sharing.

Knowledge graphs Ontologies Interoperability

Co-author · Semantic data integration

MetaUnionSearch

Used metadata alone to find unionable restricted-access tables, enabling discovery without exposing underlying data values. The method achieved 81% unionability accuracy and outperformed existing benchmarks in precision and recall.

Metadata FAIR data Search
Read the paper ->

Co-author · LLM metadata enrichment

MetaLinker

Compared three LLMs for zero-shot classification of column headers against a controlled topic vocabulary. ChatGPT and Gemini were more consistent and aligned better with human labels than Bard; dataset descriptions did not significantly change results.

About

About

I am a Knowledge Engineering and AI expert with a PhD in Computer Science. My research focuses on neurosymbolic AI, especially combining multimodal models with ontologies and knowledge graphs to connect perception and language with grounded reasoning and action.

My research includes a neurosymbolic framework combining multimodal language models and knowledge graphs for robotic perception and action, as well as semantic approaches to metadata enrichment and restricted-access data integration. This work brings together empirical AI research and knowledge engineering: models provide flexible interpretation, while ontologies and knowledge graphs make domain concepts explicit and reusable.

At UMC Utrecht, I design knowledge architectures and semantic models for clinical operations. Previously, at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, I researched neurosymbolic AI and semantic data systems. Across these roles, I have worked with interdisciplinary teams on complex, real-world problems where reliable representations and clear communication matter.
